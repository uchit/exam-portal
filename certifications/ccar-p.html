CCAR-P — Claude Certified Architect — Professional

The advanced companion to CCA-F, for architects operating past foundational agentic design into production-scale system decisions, governance, and cross-team enablement.

StatusThis certification is live and administered by Anthropic via Pearson VUE. Free practice content for it isn't published on thatclaude.com yet — we build one track at a time, fully; CCA-F is live today. This page has the domain blueprint to help you start planning, and we'll link real practice content here as it ships. Watch the changelog for updates.

Exam format

  • 63 items · 120 minutes
  • Pass mark 720/1000 · $175 per attempt
  • Pearson VUE delivery · credential valid 12 months · retake waits of 14/30/90 days (max 4 attempts/year) · free non-proctored renewal while current

Who it's for

Senior architects, AI/ML engineers, and technical leads designing production-scale Claude systems.

Recommended, not required: 3+ years in architecture/platform roles and 6+ months hands-on with Claude or LLM systems.
